# 050 — Image HUD (lane icons + car bars bitmap)

Status: ACTIVE
Depends on: 020 (built together with 030's automated criteria in one task since 2026-09-04)
Design reference: docs/BUILD_PLAN.md §3, §7 Phase 4; skill `g2-hud-display`

## Purpose

Replace the small text glyph with a large, glanceable symbol and a real bar, rendered as one 288×144 gray4 image, while keeping text mode as the automatic fallback.

## Scope

In: `drawHud(state) → Uint8Array`, gray4 packing, `updateImageRawData` path in the queue, coalescing at 250 ms, stale half-intensity rendering, automatic fallback after 3 consecutive `sendFailed`, SDK quirk shim, latency logging.
Out: more than four image containers, icons for messages. (The message blink is the only animation, by human decision 2026-10-01; the lane-call blink of 2026-09-30/10-01 was removed on 2026-10-28 — a call is solid at once.)

## Requirements

R1. MUST follow the bitmap layout in the `g2-hud-display` skill (lane icon row, three car bars, level-3 alert outline, stale halving).
R2. MUST issue at most one flush per 250 ms for cars-only changes (≤ 2 sends: BL+BR); lane changes bypass the debounce; a pending job for an image container is replaced, never queued behind another job for that container.
R3. MUST switch to text mode after 3 consecutive `sendFailed` and stay there until restart.
R4. Nibble order and row stride MUST be confirmed on hardware with a test pattern and recorded in `docs/ENVIRONMENT.md`.
R5. MUST require Even App ≥ 2.2.7 for image mode (warn on the companion page otherwise) because of the LZ4 regression. The simulator does not decode LZ4 and does not enforce on-device image-size limits, so neither can be verified there.
R6. Image mode MUST be the default everywhere (hardware and simulator); text mode only via `?render=text`, the stored override, or the `sendFailed` fallback.

## Decisions

- 2026-09-03 One image for symbol+bar — why: image cost is per call (~185 ms), not per byte.
- 2026-09-03 Simulator evidence (`[SIM]`) covers bitmap content and queue behaviour; size limits, nibble order, pacing and `sendFailed` fallback stay `[HW]` — why: the simulator explicitly does not enforce on-device image limits or LZ4 and is faster than hardware.

- 2026-09-04 (Maxx) Image is the primary path from day one (no text-first phase). `drawHud` is composed from primitives (`fillRect`, `fillTriangle`, `fillCircle`, `hline`, `vline`, `pixel`) in `apps/glasses/src/render/primitives.ts`; the layout lives in `apps/glasses/src/render/hud-design.ts` as the only file to edit when changing the look. The golden snapshot in AC-1 pins the *current* design — a deliberate design change updates the golden in the same commit.
- 2026-09-04 (Maxx) The simulator is the primary functional check for the bitmap (AC-5b); R4/AC-6 nibble-order confirmation and AC-7/AC-8 pacing remain hardware questions to be discussed later.

- 2026-09-04 (Maxx) **Design round 1 — three lane positions, dither, side arrows.** The single 288×144 bitmap is re-laid out: three fixed lane slots across the top in track order (▼ at x 48, ● at x 144, ▲ at x 240) with the called one filled and the other two drawn as 2 px outlines at level 4, so the driver always sees where a call would appear; the car-behind bar in the middle band (y 76–107, same outline/fill/tick/≥90-alert rules); and a bottom band (y 112–142) holding a ◀ at the left edge for `side: 'inside'` or a ▶ at the right edge for `side: 'outside'`. **Every filled area is dithered** with a 2×2 checkerboard (`{on:15, off:6}`, alert fill `{on:8, off:3}`) — "easier on the eyes"; outlines and ticks stay flat. The 1-pixel period was chosen over a 4×4 Bayer cell because it dissolves into an even tone at these shape sizes instead of showing texture. NO-LINK dimming stays `v >> 1` over the whole frame. AC-1's goldens are re-recorded for this design, plus one `min`-sampled golden that makes the dither itself visible.

- 2026-09-25 (Maxx) **Design round 3 — half-size icons on top, three car bars on the bottom.** Maxx: "icons are too large … make them 1/2 current size. replace middle circle with dash/rectangle. move icons to top of screen … on bottom of screen have 3 hollow bars. left, middle, right these represent cars behind and closeness." The bitmap's top row holds ▼ ▬ ▲ at x 48 / 144 / 240 (triangles 30 px tall and 34 wide, the middle a 40×10 dash; called = dithered fill, others 2 px outlines at level 4). Its bottom row holds three hollow 86×28 bars (y 114–141, x 5 / 101 / 197, centred under the icons), each 2 px outline at level 6 split into three 26-px cells; `cars[i]` cells are dithered-filled **left-to-right** (chosen to match the spotter's `1 · 2 · 3` segments; recorded as `DESIGN.cars.fillDirection`), and a bar at level 3 swaps to outline 15 + alert fill `{8,3}`. NO-LINK stays `v >> 1`. **Constraint Maxx may want to revisit:** only ONE image container (≤ 288×144) exists, so "top of screen" is the top of that image at canvas y 8 and "bottom" is the bottom of the same image (canvas y ≈ 122–150); the message text now sits below the image (y 160) and the status strip stays bottom-right. Putting the bars at the very bottom of the 576×288 canvas would need a second image container (costs ~100 ms per update and is ruled out by constitution §3) — say so if that trade is wanted. AC-1/AC-2/AC-4 are rewritten to the new elements (human-authorized) and the goldens are re-recorded (four lanes, cars `[0,0,0]`, `[1,2,3]`, `[3,0,0]`, dim, and the `min`-sampled dither map).

- 2026-09-25 (Maxx) **Design round 4 — icons and bars on the perimeter, four edge-strip images.** Maxx: "I want the icons to be close to the perimeter so it's not directly in line of sight. Separate the bars on the glasses. Use corners on the glasses, then the top border and bottom borders for the 'middle' icons." The pinned SDK 0.0.12 caps a page at 4 image containers (each ≤ 288×144), 8 text and 12 total (`docs/RESEARCH_NOTES.md` §2), so six separate images are impossible and one image cannot reach the perimeter. The HUD becomes two 576×48 strips drawn once each and split at x 288 into four pixel-adjacent 288×48 image containers — `stripTL` (0,0), `stripTR` (288,0), `stripBL` (0,240), `stripBR` (288,240). Top strip: ▼ in the top-left corner (x 51), ▬ centred on the seam (x 288), ▲ in the top-right corner (x 525). Bottom strip: LEFT bar in the bottom-left corner, MIDDLE bar straddling the seam, RIGHT bar in the bottom-right corner (x 8 / 245 / 482, each 86×28, under the icon of its side). Shapes, sizes, dither, outline and alert rules are unchanged from round 3; NO-LINK halves every strip, so all four images dim. The renderer is `drawTopStrip`/`drawBottomStrip` + `splitStrip`, all pure; goldens are per strip plus a seam test. The queue keys jobs by image container: a newer job replaces the pending one, bytes equal to the last accepted send are skipped (lane change ≤ 2 sends, left/right car 1, middle car ≤ 2, stale clear only what changed), the car strip is debounced per strip (both halves flush together), the lane strip is immediate, logs are `{call, ms, result, container}`, and 3× `sendFailed` still falls back to the single-text-container page. This supersedes the 2026-09-03 "one image for symbol+bar" decision, the round-3 "only ONE image container" constraint and this spec's *Scope* line "Out: more than one image container" (left for the planner to reword, with constitution §3). **Per-call hardware cost of four images is unverified** (`[HW]` T109/T110). AC-1/AC-3/AC-4 are rewritten to per-container bitmaps and AC-5b/AC-6/AC-7 say "each image container" (T057).
- 2026-09-30 (Maxx) Dithering off — solid fills (15; alert 8) — why: Maxx asked for it after seeing the simulator; the dither was an unverified guess about waveguide glare.
- 2026-09-30 (Maxx) **Design round 5 — ◀ ▲ ▶ lane icons that blink on a call, corner "banana / L" car bars, a vertical middle bar.** Maxx: "For bottom sliders, is it possible to do bars on the corners with rounded edges? Kinda look like a mix between a banana and an L. Then for middle still be a bar but it can be vertical instead of horizontal. Can use segments instead of dynamic sliders on the glasses. For the top bars, make the left triangle point left, right triangle point right, make middle a triangle pointing up. For those, when the user presses the app button on the phone make the glasses image blink. Empty outline then filled outline." Top strip unchanged in size (2 × 288×48): `◀` (bottom lane) at the far left, `▲` (middle lane) on the seam, `▶` (top lane) at the far right, round-3 size (30 px apex to base, 34 across); called = solid 15, others 2 px outlines at 4. **Blink:** when the applied `state.lane` changes to a lane, the called icon is drawn outline (2 px, level 15) → filled → outline → filled, `LANE_BLINK_MS` = 300 ms per phase on the injected timer (`apps/glasses/src/blink.ts`), ending filled; a newer lane, a clear, NO LINK or `stop()` cancels it; each phase is a lane-immediate top-strip job, so a call costs up to 3 extra sends for ◀/▶ and 6 for ▲ (it straddles the seam) — **hardware cost unverified**, recorded in the skill for `[HW]` T109/T110. **Bottom containers grow to 288×96** at y 192 (`stripBL` (0,192), `stripBR` (288,192); SDK max 288×144): a virtual 576×96 canvas split at x 288, packed to 13 824 bytes per container. LEFT = a quarter ring centred on the canvas corner (local (0,96)), radii 60–88, swept from straight up to straight right, three 30° segments with 2 px parallel-sided gaps; RIGHT = its pixel mirror; MIDDLE = a vertical 28×85 bar on the seam, three 27-px segments. Every segment is its own 2 px outline (6) with a solid fill (15) inset 1 px; segments fill **from the bottom edge up** (`fillDirection: 'bottom-up'`, the spotter faders' direction — for the rings, from the horizontal end towards the vertical one); level 3 → outline 15 + fill 8. `fillRingSector`/`strokeRingSector` (pixel-centre rasterisation) join `primitives.ts`; every position stays in `hud-design.ts`. AC-1/AC-2 are rewritten for the new shapes (human-authorized); AC-3's "6 912 bytes" now holds for the two top containers only — the bottom ones pack to 13 824 (`gray4.test.ts`), left for the planner to reword.
- 2026-09-30 (Maxx) design round 5b — corner rings curve into the corners; triangles 42×42 (built 42 × 42 between vertices, 43 lit rows; see below) — Maxx: "Flip the corners. I want the rounded to be down towards the corners. Also make triangles larger." The corner car bars are still three 30° segments (2 px gaps, outline 6, fill inset 1, bottom-edge arm first, level 3 → outline 15 + fill 8) but each quarter ring is now centred INSIDE the strip — local (R, 96 − R) = (88, 8) for LEFT, (488, 8) for RIGHT, R = outer radius 88, inner radius 60 — over the quadrant facing the corner (180°→270° / 270°→360°), so the outer arc is tangent to the side and bottom edges (lit x 0–86 / 489–575, y 9–95) and the arms point up the side edge and along the bottom edge; the middle bar is unchanged. Lane triangles grow from 30×34 to 42 px along the pointing axis × 42 px between the base vertices (43 lit rows, rows 3–45 of the 48 px strip): a full 44 px span would leave 1 px below, and the brief asked for ≥ 2 px margins; blink outline thickness unchanged and blink send counts unchanged. Design-only: `hud-design.ts` + goldens; `primitives.ts` already handled any angle range.

- 2026-10-01 (Maxx) design round 6 — approved from mocks `docs/reviews/hud-design-round6.png` + `docs/reviews/spotter-round6.png` before any code. (a) **Lane blink**: "pushing the button should make the glasses image blink every .5 seconds for 4 seconds" — `LANE_BLINK_MS` 500, eight phases outline/filled ×4, ends filled; cancel rules unchanged. (b) **Message in the image**: "put text upper middle below the top rectangle … make text larger and centered, like 3x the current size … make text blink every second for 5 seconds" — the SDK text container has a fixed font, so in image mode the message is drawn as a BITMAP into the top strip, which grows from 576×48 to 576×96 (two 288×96 images): a 5×7 bitmap font scaled ×6 (30×42 px glyphs, 6 px advance gap, upper-case + digits + basic punctuation; unknown characters → `?`), centred on x 288 under the ▲, rows ≈ 51–93, truncated with the last glyph replaced by `…`-equivalent (`.`) if it would exceed the strip. Visible 1 s, hidden 1 s, visible, hidden, visible, then cleared and auto-acked at `MSG_AUTO_ACK_MS` (5 s) exactly as today (`pendingAckMsgId` retry unchanged). Lane icons keep rows 3–45. The `msg` text container is text-mode only. (c) **Car bars**: "make bottom middle bar shorter and corner bars longer & taller but same width" on "I want the curved things like it was before … extend the bars touching the bottom and sides … longer 3 segment rectangle with outlines … expands from the center out … 1 rectangle shorter so there is more difference between 1 and 2". Bottom strip grows to 576×144 (two 288×144 images, the SDK maximum; `STRIP_Y.bottom` 144). LEFT/RIGHT = an L flush with the side and bottom edges: arms 136 px long (up the side, along the bottom) × 28 px thick, inner elbow a concave fillet of radius 60 centred at local (88, 56) / (488, 56); split into three segments of EQUAL AREA along the L (bottom arm first, then the elbow, then the side arm) by cutting the elbow region at angles chosen numerically; 2 px outlines level 6, 1 px fill inset, 2 px gaps, level 3 → outline 15 + fill 8, all as before. MIDDLE = one horizontal 180×28 bar centred on the seam, bottom edge 4 px above the strip bottom, 2 px outline with two 2 px internal dividers making cells 70 / 40 / 70; the fill grows symmetrically from the centre: level 1 → 40 px (the centre cell), level 2 → 110 px, level 3 → 180 px (inside the outline, 1 px inset). The `status` text container moves up to y 108 so it stays clear of the 144 px strip. Cars images are ~50 % larger per send — pacing unverified on hardware (T051).

- 2026-10-28 (Maxx) **Design round 7 — 50 % larger solid lane triangles, the message between the corner car bars.** Approved from the mock "option A" ("message between the car bars, same big text as today … triangles 50% larger, solid"); preview from the real drawers `docs/reviews/hud-design-round7.png`. (1) **Lane triangles 50 % larger**: `DESIGN.lanes.length` 42 → 63 (the apex 32 px from `centreY`, the base 31 — whole-pixel vertices), `halfWidth` 21 → 32 (31.5 rounded), `centreY` 24 → 35, centres x 51 / 288 / 525 unchanged: ▲ lit x 256–320 / rows 3–66, ◀ x 19–82 and ▶ x 494–557 / rows 3–67 (≥ 2 px from every edge); uncalled lanes stay faint 2 px outlines at level 4. (2) **No lane-call blink**: a call shows the FILLED triangle in the very frame that applies it and it stays filled ("solid instead of blinking"); `src/blink.ts`, `LANE_BLINK_MS`, the `outline` lane style and `blinkOutlineLevel` are removed, so a call costs ≤ 2 top-strip sends in all (▲ TL + TR, ◀ TL, ▶ TR; ◀ ↔ ▶ TL + TR) instead of 8–16. (3) **The message moves from the top strip to the bottom strips**: same glyphs (5×7 font ×6 = 30×42 px, 6 px letter gap, level 15), centred on x 288 in the band between the two corner Ls and above the middle bar; up to two lines with an 8 px line gap at bottom-strip rows 6–47 and 56–97 (canvas y 150–241), a single line vertically centred (rows 31–72). Line breaking: split on `\n` first (the phone sends e.g. `"GO LOW\nEXIT"` — line 1 the action, line 2 the corner phase), before the font's normalisation (which would draw `\n` as `?`); a line still wider than 288 px — the 304 px between the Ls' bounding boxes (x 136–439) less 8 px clearance each side, i.e. 8 glyphs = 282 px — is word-wrapped greedily; at most 2 lines: everything after line 1 joins line 2, and a line still too wide (a word longer than 8 glyphs, the joined overflow) is cut with the existing `.` truncation char. Measured on the real drawers: ≥ 18 dark px from the corner Ls (the fillet keeps the L far from the band), 14 dark rows above the middle bar. `"CATCHING UP"` → CATCHING / UP, `"LEADERS BEHIND"` → LEADERS / BEHIND, `"PULLING AWAY"` → PULLING / AWAY, `"PITS"` one line. The top strip now holds only the lane icons (still 576×96 — the triangles need 68 rows; container layout unchanged). (4) **Message timing unchanged** (visible 1 s / hidden 1 s for 5 s, then cleared + auto-acked; keeps blinking dimmed on NO LINK) — it now re-renders the bottom strip: the queue sends a change of the message drawn (new, cleared, every blink phase) to BL + BR immediately, not after the 250 ms cars debounce; a lane change never touches the bottom strip and a message phase never the top. Caches: the top strip is repacked only when lane/linkOk change; the bottom strip when cars/linkOk/the drawn message change, and the costly car bars (corner-L regions) are redrawn only when the cars change — a message phase or link change lays the message over a copy of the kept car layer (`drawCarsLayer` + `composeBottomStrip`; `queue-draw-cost.test.ts`). A message costs 6 frames × BL + BR = 12 bottom sends over its 5 s, as the top strip did in round 6 — but each is now a 288×144 image (~185 ms estimated) rather than 288×96 (~158 ms), **pacing unverified on hardware** (T109/T110, T051). (5) **Text-mode fallback** (after 3 failed image sends) and text mode: the `msg` text container shows the message with each `\n` replaced by a space. (6) The image page stays 4 image strips + `status` + `bg`; no new containers. AC-1/AC-4 reworded to this design (human-authorized); goldens re-recorded in the same commit.
